        Operating model

        Scan. Blueprint. Pilot. Measure. Improve.

        The goal is not to install the maximum amount of AI. The goal is to remove avoidable work while preserving the evidence, control, and judgment that the process actually needs.

        01
        Scan
        Map the current workflow, people, systems, inputs, outputs, bottlenecks, search time, handoffs, failure modes, and business consequences.
        02
        Blueprint
        Define the future-state workflow: deterministic automation, agent/model roles, permissions, evidence needs, escalation paths, and human decision ownership.
        03
        Pilot
        Build the smallest useful working version against a controlled set of real or representative cases rather than a broad platform first.
        04
        Measure
        Compare the old and new workflow using observable measures such as cycle time, human touches, search time, completeness, rework, handoff quality, and critical errors.
        05
        Improve
        Use measured failures, user feedback, and reviewed outcomes to refine prompts, controls, tools, routing, and process design without silently changing the evidence standard.
        Enterprise workflow patterns

        Representative workflow patterns the operating model can support.

        These are representative system-design patterns, not a services catalog. The implementation may be deterministic automation, supervised model assistance, evidence retrieval, or a technical agent depending on risk and evidence needs.

        IN
        Intake & request handling

        Turn messy incoming requests into structured, action-ready cases with owners, missing information, priority, deadlines, and review gates.

        RF
        Technical RFP / RFI & proposal operations

        Decompose requirements, track evidence and owners, identify gaps, prepare technical response material, and preserve review/QC.

        RS
        Research & evidence workflows

        Accelerate research, source review, evidence mapping, uncertainty handling, technical drafting, and human-verified conclusions.

        RP
        Recurring reports & decision briefs

        Collect inputs, detect missing information, summarize changes, surface exceptions, and prepare decision-ready material for human review.

        TE
        Technical engineering workflows

        Connect tools, measurements, scripts, test evidence, troubleshooting steps, and supervised AI without handing unrestricted control to a model.

        EV
        Agent evaluation & guardrails

        Build test cases, failure taxonomies, human-verified evaluation judgments, regression checks, routing/depth controls, and explicit evidence boundaries for agent behavior.

        Design principles

        Automation only helps when the operating model survives contact with reality.

        EVOLVE is built around a few non-negotiables that reduce the chance of a convincing demo turning into an unreliable business process.

        01. Evidence firstOutputs should show where important facts came from and what remains uncertain.
        02. Permission is separate from confidenceA model can be confident and still not be authorized to act.
        03. Human judgment stays visibleHigh-consequence, ambiguous, or irreversible decisions remain explicitly human-owned.
        04. Measure before claiming ROICycle time and labor savings should come from observed workflow comparisons, not marketing arithmetic.
        05. Learn from reviewed failuresContinuous improvement should be driven by traceable outcomes and regression-tested changes.
